Privacy

Draft — last updated 12 November 2026. To be reviewed before TechPlug POS is sold commercially.

What the app stores

TechPlug POS stores your restaurant’s data — menu, prices, orders, payments, change owed, stock, purchases, expenses, staff names, roles and PINs — on the device where you use it.

What we receive

Today, nothing from your restaurant’s records is sent to us. When online backup and sharing between devices are switched on in a later version, your data will be stored with our database provider (Supabase) so your devices can share it. Each restaurant’s data will be kept separate and visible only to that restaurant’s logins.

Customers

If you record a customer’s name or phone number (for change owed, delivery or online orders), you are responsible for having a good reason to keep it and for deleting it when it is no longer needed.

Website

This website does not use advertising trackers. Download counts are provided by GitHub, where the installer is published.

Your choices

You can export, change or delete your restaurant’s data at any time from the app. Contact us to close an account.
